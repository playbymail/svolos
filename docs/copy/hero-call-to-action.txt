Your colony ship has finally reached its destination—but decades late, crippled, and dying. The engines are burned out, critical systems are failing, and the technology that carried you across the stars can no longer be maintained. Thousands of colonists are awake, frightened, and depending on you to get them safely to the surface.

The world below should have been ready. Advance expeditions established mines, farms, factories, fuel stores, and supply depots before your arrival. Their work remains. They do not. No transmissions. No ships. No sign of the people who were supposed to greet you. You have inherited their resources, their silence, and whatever happened to them.

Now the colony is yours to command. Decide what to build, what to mine, what to salvage, and what to sacrifice. Rebuild from the wreckage, explore the unknown, and lead your people from survival toward a new civilization among the stars. **Take command of your colony and begin the challenge.**
